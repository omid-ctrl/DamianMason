Damian Mason speaker photos
===========================

These photographs were recovered from Damian Mason's first-party website and
media archive for use by meeting planners, event organizers, and publications.

The archive contains studio portraits and live-event photographs in their
available source resolution. File names describe the image; no client logos or
the retired B.A.F. mark are included.

No photographer credit or usage license survived in the supplied files. Before
commercial publication, contact damianmasonoffice@gmail.com to confirm any
credit, crop, or usage requirements.
