- price on old site: $19.95 (must NOT appear on new site)

- Duplicate content on the page: the short description above the cart and the Description tab are byte-identical. The same copy appears a third time as the `og:description` and a fourth time as the featured image `alt`.
- The featured image `alt` attribute is the entire 4-sentence product description (400+ characters) instead of an image description. Accessibility defect.
- The WooCommerce gallery renders the same file (`FoodFear-Mockup-Online-Store.png`) twice, producing a two-thumbnail gallery of one identical image.
- Two conflicting `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD blocks on one page: Yoast says Home > Shop > Food Fear, the commerce block says Home > Books > Food Fear.
- The first paragraph of the description is not description copy at all, it is the product title restated. Reads as a formatting mistake.
- Single-tab tab strip ("Description") with nothing to switch to.
- No purchase link to any external retailer. There is no Amazon, Audible, Barnes & Noble or Bookshop URL anywhere in the mirrored source for this book.
- No author bio, endorsement, blurb, review, ISBN, publisher, page count or publication date. The page has no credibility content beyond the description paragraph.
- `Product` JSON-LD hardcodes `priceValidUntil` / `validThrough` of 2027-12-31 and `availability: InStock`.
- WooCommerce/Stripe payment scaffolding is inlined into the page (WooPay express checkout button, Stripe express-checkout element, live Stripe publishable key in a config blob).
- Header logo `<img>` and all footer links point at `damianmason.wpengine.com` (staging host).
- Site-wide header nav has empty `href=""` on "Speaking", "Podcasts" and "Media".
- Secondary bar exposes a live cart counter ("0 Items" -> /cart/) and a raw Gmail address (damianmasonoffice@gmail.com).
