## Defects observed on the live page
- Eyebrow heading reads "COllaborate with damian" — broken capitalization (two leading caps, lowercase proper noun "damian"). Verbatim on the live site.
- Typo: "complimentary industries" in the PODCAST HOST & GUEST card — should be "complementary".
- Typo: "knowledgable commentary" in the NEWS & COMMENTARY card — should be "knowledgeable".
- `Screenshot-2023-04-18-at-5.52.49-PM.png` — a macOS screenshot with its raw default filename used as a full-bleed parallax marketing band, with no alt text and no content in the section at all.
- Self-hosted `dm_-_innovation-1080p.mp4` is **39 MB**, served from `damianmason.wpengine.com`, with no poster frame, no captions, and no lazy loading.
- Page has no `og:image` — link previews will render with no artwork.
- The hero intro paragraph is marked up as `<h6>` inside a `<blockquote>` — a heading level used for body copy, breaking the heading outline (H2 → H6 → H1).
- Heading order is inverted in the hero: the H2 eyebrow precedes the only H1 on the page.
- Two H2s inside a single CTA module ("Damian Mason is a Businessman…" and "How can we work together?").
- "Contact Damian today!" uses a relative href `/contact-us/` while all five other CTAs use absolute `https://damianmason.com/...` — inconsistent and fragile.
- The "Want to join the conversation?" section carries `et_clickable` with no `data-link` — a dead whole-section click target.
- CTA labelled "Have a question? Email us!" links to `/contact-us/`, not a `mailto:` — label does not match behaviour.
- Mailing-list CTA points at `/join-mailing-list/` while the identical CTA on /reviews/ points at `/join-the-conversation/` — two competing signup pages.
- Google SERP CSS class `hgKElc` left on all six checkmark bullets — copy was pasted straight out of Google search results.
- Leftover Squarespace markup inside the Divi testimonial modules: `sqs-block quote-block sqs-block-quote null sqs-col-10 span-10 float float-right`, `data-block-type="31"`, `figure class="block-animation-none"`, `<figcaption class="source">`, and stale `block-yui_3_17_2_1_1527781697578_*` DOM IDs.
- Divi testimonial author/position/company fields are all empty (`et_pb_testimonial_meta` renders blank) — attribution is faked with pasted HTML instead.
- First testimonial ends with a dangling en dash: `“I would highly recommend working with Damian!” –`
- First testimonial's opening curly quote sits INSIDE the first `<span>` while its closing quote sits in a second span with the stray dash — inconsistent with the other two.
- Third testimonial is followed by a stray empty `<p><span></span></p>`.
- Contact form uses `type="text"` for both the email and phone fields — no native validation, no mobile keyboard hinting.
- Contact form field order is Email → Full Name → Phone → Message; every field duplicates its label text as its placeholder.
- Contact form posts to the page URL with a WordPress nonce — no reusable endpoint; needs replacing wholesale.
- Empty `<p>&nbsp;</p>` spacer inside the collaboration CTA module.
- Empty `<strong></strong>` at the end of the PODCAST HOST & GUEST card.
- Doubled and leading non-breaking spaces in the PODCAST HOST & GUEST copy and a trailing non-breaking space on "Want to join the conversation? ".
- Credential bullets are `<p>` + literal `•` + `<br />` rather than real `<ul>`/`<li>` — inaccessible list semantics, repeated four times.
- Four decorative FontAwesome glyphs are injected as text characters inside `<span class="et-pb-icon">` with no accessible name.
- Four base64-inlined SVG section dividers bloat the CSS payload for a purely decorative diagonal wedge.
- Header logo and every footer link point at `damianmason.wpengine.com` / `?page_id=NNN` (site-wide, not page-specific).
- Page ships an unusually large DOM (356 KB of HTML) for a single marketing page.
