## Defects observed on the live page
- Missing meta description.
- Duplicate H1: the page H1 is "News & Media" and the Forbes item is also an H1 ("Damian Quoted In Forbes") — every other media item is an H2.
- Five empty `<h1><strong></strong></h1>` elements are used as vertical spacers above "Damian Quoted In Forbes".
- An empty Divi video module (`et_pb_video_0`) sits above the Soybeans image and renders as a blank gap.
- An entirely empty Divi section (`et_pb_section_1`) closes the page.
- Three press images load from `damianmason.wpengine.com` (staging host) instead of `damianmason.com`: Screenshot-2023-04-25-at-10.51.10-AM.png, -10.52.49-AM.png, -10.56.20-AM.png.
- The section background image `orange-background-2.png` also loads from `damianmason.wpengine.com`.
- Two 2024 image URLs contain a raw, unencoded U+202F NARROW NO-BREAK SPACE inside the filename (`...11.48.25 AM.png`, `...11.49.00 AM.png`) — invalid in an unescaped URL and hostile to any static-asset pipeline.
- All 6 press images are raw macOS screenshots (untrimmed browser chrome / player UI) used as marketing imagery.
- All 6 press images have `alt=""` — zero accessible text for any press item.
- No dates on any of the 9 media items; the Eagle Country item references an "upcoming event" that is years past.
- Trailing space inside `<strong>Damian Mason on Straight Arrow News: </strong>` on both Straight Arrow News items.
- The second Straight Arrow News eyebrow H2 is missing the `post-title text-name1` class the first one has — inconsistent class usage.
- Heading case is inconsistent across the page: Straight Arrow News / Cheddar items are sentence case, the Forbes item is Title Case.
- "NewsMaxTV" (on-page heading) vs "NewsmaxTV" (iframe title) — the brand is spelled two ways.
- Two of the nine media items duplicate the two blog posts (Eggflation article, Cheddar climate video) at different URLs with differently-cased titles.
- The OG image (`331668334_973198694086419_2283442893014987447_n.jpeg`, a Facebook-export filename) does not appear anywhere on the page.
- Only the Forbes item has a readable text CTA; the other eight items are click-the-image-and-hope, with no visible affordance.
- Site-wide (noted once): footer links resolve to `https://damianmason.wpengine.com/?page_id=...` staging URLs, and the header "Speaking", "Podcasts" and "Media" top-level items have EMPTY HREFs.
- Sized derivatives referenced in `srcset` (e.g. `-1280x722`, `-980x553`, `-480x271`) are not all present in `_source/media/`; only the full-size originals were mirrored.
