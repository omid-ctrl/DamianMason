# Display typeface decision

Cool Modern Ag revision. Four comps, one control, one winner.

Comps are generated by `scripts/build-type-comps.mjs` and shot by `scripts/shoot.mjs` at 390 and
1440, **at `--dpr 1`**. The 1x part is deliberate: every screenshot in this project's history was
taken at 2x, which is exactly the density at which a hairline that falls under one device pixel
still has two to land on. The defect that set the outgoing face's 40px floor had never once been
photographed.

All four comps are byte-identical except for one object in the generator, so the shots register
pixel-for-pixel and a judge can flip between them. The Phase 1a direction bake-off could not do that
(three different layouts, compared from memory) and the legibility lens lost that round partly
because of it.

Comps render on the **new** cool-white palette. Judging a face against a ground it will never ship
on is judging the wrong thing.

## Winner: Oswald 600

Vernon Adams' reworking of ATF **Alternate Gothic**: the actual American newspaper and poster gothic
lineage, not something gothic-adjacent.

| | lc advance | cap height | vs Archivo body | latin KB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bodoni Moda 500 *(control, shipped)* | 0.539 | 0.750 | almost none | 25.2 |
| **Oswald 600** | **0.441** | **0.810** | **-18% width, +18% cap** | **12.5** |
| Big Shoulders 600 | 0.440 | 0.800 | -19% width | 56.9 |
| Libre Franklin 800 | 0.556 | 0.742 | +3% width | 15.6 |
| Archivo 700 *(the body face)* | 0.540 | 0.686 | n/a | n/a |

### The three reasons, in order of how much they mattered

**1. Fit, and it is measurable.** The home page H1 broke mid-word at 1024, 1200, 1280, 1380 and
1440. `app/page.tsx` called that "KNOWN, MEASURED, AND NOT FIXABLE HERE" and `--fs-6xl-hero` was
fitted to `Coordinators` instead, because a Didone cannot carry `Straight-Forward` in a 7-of-12
track at any size the ladder permits. After the swap and a re-derived ramp: **CHOP 0**.

**2. Register.** A Didone is Vogue and Harper's: refinement and distance. The headline underneath it
reads "Straight-Forward Agriculture Dialogue" and the buyer is a farm credit executive. The face was
arguing with the copy.

**3. Differentiation from the body face.** The risk this bake-off existed to test: Archivo is
already a grotesk, and two grotesks that look alike muddy every page. Oswald separates on **two
independent axes at once**, width and cap height. That is what a newspaper does, a condensed
headline over normal-width text.

## Why the other two lost

**Libre Franklin was never meant to win.** Historically it is the *most* correct answer to "American
newspaper gothic" (Benton's Franklin Gothic), and it is **normal width**. It was carried through to
make the width thesis falsifiable rather than merely asserted: if differentiation could be bought on
weight alone, it was cheaper and more literate. It could not. At 800 it is **wider than the Didone**,
it breaks `Straight-Forward` at the hyphen, next to Archivo it reads as one face in two weights, and
its comp runs **5489px against Oswald's 4619px** for identical content. The thesis is proven, not
assumed.

**Big Shoulders lost on measured cost.** It is the best-looking option in the sample and renders
almost identically to Oswald at headline sizes. But `weight: ['600']` silently returns the **opsz-10
text master**, wrongly modulated at 110px, and getting the real display master needs `axes: ['opsz']`,
which forces `weight: 'variable'` and pulls **56.9 KB** onto the file that gates LCP. That is 4.6x
Oswald for no visible gain, and it is the same class of defect this repo already paid for once with
the Source Serif italic.

## The objection, and the answer

Oswald is a top-20 Google font welded to 2014-era all-caps hero overlays. Three things defuse it,
and this system already does all three: it is set at **600**, not the ubiquitous 400; it is **never
all-caps** (all-caps Oswald *is* the cliché, and uppercase here is already spoken for by IBM Plex
Mono); and it is set title-case with tight tracking on a cool stone ground.

## Results

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-word heading breaks (CHOP) | 5 | **0** |
| Preloaded font bytes | 128.7 KB / 5 files | **115.9 KB / 5 files** |
| Display face alone | 25.2 KB | **12.5 KB** |
| `--fs-6xl-hero` | 92 to 110px, fitted to `Coordinators` | 83 to 109px, fitted to `Straight-Forward` |

## What did not survive

- **`--ls-tightest`.** -0.032em was 5.9% of Bodoni's lowercase advance but 7.3% of Oswald's, a 24%
  harder squeeze on a face whose sidebearings are minimal by construction. Split into
  `--ls-display` and `--ls-display-masthead`, because one value cannot serve 34px and 120px.
- **`--lh-solid`.** Its only consumer, `.dm-display--masthead`, was never applied in any `.tsx`, and
  the utility it minted was never used in markup.
- **`font-optical-sizing: auto`.** It never did anything. `next/font` only requests an `opsz` axis
  if you pass `axes:`, this app does not, so the shipped Bodoni file had no optical-size master to
  drive. Five declarations were controlling nothing, and `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` section 2 described a
  mechanism that was not in the bytes. Oswald has no `opsz` axis, so the question is now closed
  rather than silently unanswered.
- **The `DisplaySize` size floor, as a legibility rule.** It was written because a Didone sheds
  strokes below 40px. A condensed gothic has none to lose. But the gate was quietly doing a second
  job, keeping the masthead voice off the section-head step, so it survives as a **rank** rule:
  `display` now requires `level: 1`, and the size floor drops one step to `3xl` rather than
  disappearing, because `2xl` **is** the H2 step.

## All four families stay

I expected to argue Source Serif 4 was a leftover once a gothic arrived. It is not, and the reason
is mechanical: **no condensed gothic on Google Fonts has working tabular figures.** Oswald, Big
Shoulders, Anton, Libre Franklin, Antonio, Saira Condensed and Archivo Narrow all leave figures
proportional even when asked for `tabular-nums`. Source Serif's are tabular by default, and
`globals.css` applies tabular lining figures to the stat ledger and every episode and press date.

So `--family-figure` cannot move, and "do not set a proof figure in the display face" gets a better
reason than the one it had: not "the Didone's hairlines are fragile", which is a face-specific
accident, but **"the display face has no tabular figures, and a ledger column that does not align is
not a ledger."** That applies to every future display face, which is what a design-system rule
should do.

The register argument lands the same way. Under Bodoni the system carried two serifs differing
mainly in contrast, so a reader mostly saw "serif". Removing the Didone removed the duplicate, not
the serif. What is left is four families separated on four **independent** axes: display by width,
serif by contrast, body by normal width, mono by rhythm. And the swap test still bites: condensed
gothic plus grotesk plus mono with no serif anywhere **is** the Series B SaaS homepage this
direction was chosen against.
